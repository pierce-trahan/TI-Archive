# Team logos

Downloaded from [Liquipedia Commons](https://liquipedia.net/commons/) by `scripts/fetch-team-logos.ts`, at a single consistent width.

Each logo is the trademark of the organisation it represents. They are reproduced here to identify the teams that competed in and won the events this archive documents — the ordinary identifying use such marks are published for. They are not endorsements, and no claim of ownership is made.

**If you represent one of these organisations and want your logo removed, open an issue and it will be taken out.**
