The one that isn’t a Major

Galaxy Battles II: Emerging Worlds is the only top-tier event of the season Valve didn’t back, and the chart shows it before any of the reporting does: tier one by field and prize money, no circuit backing, the smallest pool of the bunch.

In late 2017 the Philippines’ Games and Amusement Board began treating esports professionals as athletes, which meant players competing at a local event needed a GAB licence — and that licence required drug testing, for marijuana and shabu among other substances. Galaxy Battles II was scheduled for Manila in January 2018. Weeks out, Valve revoked its Major status, saying the requirements were unreasonable infringements on the privacy of the players, as a condition to enter the country. Team Liquid, Virtus.pro and Team Secret all withdrew.

What was left still ran, and VGJ.Thunder won it — a $500,000 event with eight teams where there should have been a Major with sixteen.
