The one that isn’t a Major

Galaxy Battles II: Emerging Worlds is the only top-tier event of the season Valve didn’t back, and the chart shows it before any of the reporting does: tier one by field and prize money, no circuit backing, the smallest pool of the bunch.

It was announced in October 2017 as exactly the opposite — 16 teams, 8 direct invites, 1M. Dollars, a new Major brought to the Pro Circuit by Fallout Gaming and Purpose Win. Through November and December the field filled in normally: Team Liquid confirmed, Team Spirit through a qualifier, Team Secret and OpTic punching tickets.

Then, in late 2017, the Philippines’ Games and Amusement Board began treating esports professionals as athletes, which meant players competing at a Manila event needed a GAB licence — and that licence required drug testing. On 5 January 2018, two weeks out, Valve rescinded the event’s Major status, calling the requirements unreasonable infringements on player privacy. Virtus.pro withdrew the same day. Team Liquid and Team Secret went the next. OpTic followed on the 7th. The organisers put out a statement on the 6th; the event, they said, would go ahead regardless.

It did. OG, EG and others confirmed on the 10th, and it ran with eight teams for $500,000 — half the field and half the money it was announced with. VGJ.Thunder swept Evil Geniuses 3–0 in the grand final.
